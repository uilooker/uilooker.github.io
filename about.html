About UiLooker

Born from the frustration of testing responsive designs across multiple devices, UiLooker makes it effortless to see how your website performs on any screen size.

Our Journey

The Story Behind UiLooker

Born from real developer frustrations, UiLooker represents our commitment to making responsive design testing accessible, fast, and beautiful for everyone.

The Problem We Solved

As developers, we've all been there - frantically resizing browser windows, borrowing phones and tablets, or using clunky developer tools just to see how our websites look on different devices.

  • 50K+ Websites Tested
  • Zero Setup Required
  • Instant Results
  • 100% Free Forever

Our Simple Solution

We built UiLooker to solve this exact problem. One simple tool that shows you exactly how your website appears across all devices, instantly and beautifully.

  • 50+ Device Presets
  • Real-time Previews
  • Professional Screenshots
  • Developer-Friendly Interface

Growing Community

Today, UiLooker serves thousands of developers, designers, and agencies worldwide, making responsive design testing faster and more accessible than ever before.
